How does a mangrove project earn credits?

  1. Pick a methodology

    The rulebook for counting. For tidal wetlands under Verra’s standard, that is VM0033.

  2. Measure the baseline

    What the site would hold if nothing were done.

  3. Restore or protect

    Plant, reconnect tides, stop the clearing.

  4. Monitor and verify

    An independent auditor checks the carbon gained against the baseline.

  5. Issue credits

    Only verified tonnes become credits on a registry.

What should a panchayat ask first?

  • Who holds the rights to the land and the credits?
  • Which standard and methodology, and which version?
  • Who verifies, and how often?
  • How much of the income reaches the village?
